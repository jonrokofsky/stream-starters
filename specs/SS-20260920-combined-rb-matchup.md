# Combined RB matchup tool

## Goal

Combine the current RB profile and 2026 PFR defense-vs-RB results in one decision tool.

## Scoring

- Player profile: 40% rushing score, 25% receiving score, 35% opportunity score.
- Combined start score: 60% player profile, 40% opponent matchup.
- Rushing score includes YAC/Att at the approved 20% weight and has no age adjustment.

## Data and schedule

The RB player snapshot, PFR YAC snapshot, and PFR defense-vs-position snapshot refresh through the same Monday, Tuesday, and Friday 8 AM Eastern in-season workflow.
